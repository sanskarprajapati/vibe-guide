## Turn 1

Vibe mode is on. Type /vibe at the start of each session to keep this mode on.

I have a few quick questions so I pick tools that fit your project, not only the popular ones. Reply with a number and letter for each, like "1a 2a 3a 4b 5a 6a".

**1. Who will use it?**
- a) Just me (only you run it)
- b) Friends, family or a small group (a handful of people you know)
- c) Anyone on the internet (strangers can find and use it)

**2. Where does it run?**
- a) Only on my computer (you run it on your own laptop, nothing goes online)
- b) Online (people open it in a browser or on their phone, from anywhere)

**3. How serious is it?**
- a) Experiment or learning (fine if it breaks)
- b) Real users, free (people rely on it, but nobody pays)
- c) Paying customers (people pay you money for it)

**4. What data does it keep?**
- a) None (it doesn't save anything about people)
- b) Names, emails or logins (contact details or accounts)
- c) Payments (card details or purchase records)
- d) Sensitive (health, finances, ID numbers, or anything about kids)

**5. Monthly budget for hosting and services?**
- a) $0 (free only)
- b) Under $20 (a small, predictable amount)
- c) Flexible (you'll pay for what makes sense)

**6. Your coding experience?**
- a) Brand new (this is one of your first projects)
- b) Built a few things (you've made small apps or scripts before)
- c) I'm a developer (you write code for work or know it well)

Any answer can also be "Other", with a few words of your own.
